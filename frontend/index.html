Cryptographic Access Governance Architecture

1. AES-256-GCM Encryption Exam question papers are authenticated and encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM, preventing unauthorized plaintext disclosure before examination release.
2. Shamir's Secret Sharing (k-of-n) The master AES key is mathematically partitioned across designated approvers. Decryption is impossible without consensus meeting the threshold k.
3. Tamper-Evident Audit Ledger All access actions are permanently committed to an immutable SHA-256 hash chain, guaranteeing that any modification is detected.

Examination Access Authorization Protocol

Stage 1: Paper Encrypted & Shares Issued

The setter uploads the paper; it is encrypted with AES-256-GCM and one Shamir key share is issued per approver.

Stage 2: Threshold Approvals Registered (k-of-n)

Assigned authorities (e.g. HOD and Controller) inspect the paper and sign their respective Shamir key shares.

Stage 3: Time-Lock Window Verification

The cryptographic core verifies that current timestamp falls strictly within the authorized exam window.

Stage 4: AES Key Reconstructed & Decrypted

Shamir shares are combined in GF(256), the AES-256 key is reconstructed, and the PDF is securely served.
